# Acme Robotics — Team FAQ

Sample company documentation for the template. Replace it with your own —
anything ingested here becomes searchable by the company brain.

## Working together

- Core collaboration hours are 11:00–15:00 in the company timezone; outside
  those, work when you work best.
- Decisions live in written form. If it wasn't written down, it wasn't
  decided.
- Weekly demo is Friday at 14:00 — show something real, slides discouraged.

## Expenses

- Anything at or under $200 is approved automatically; keep the receipt for
  90 days.
- Over $200 needs manager approval before you spend.
- Software subscriptions go through the procurement list first — ask Finance
  before putting anything on a personal card.

## Time off

- Unlimited PTO with a minimum of 20 days a year; taking too little is a
  performance conversation.
- Give two weeks' notice for absences longer than three days.

## Engineering

- Everything ships behind a feature flag; long-lived branches are retired.
- On-call runs Monday to Monday, handed over in Monday standup.
- Every SEV1 and SEV2 gets a blameless postmortem within five working days.

## Security

- Hardware keys are mandatory for email, source control, and production.
- Report suspected phishing to security@acme.example within the hour — false
  alarms are always fine.
